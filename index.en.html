Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how we protect your intellectual sovereignty and personal data

What is Synapse Note, and how is it different from traditional note-taking apps?

Synapse Note is a local-first bi-directional knowledge base and AI cognitive mirror crafted for lifelong learners, researchers, and thinkers. It weaves notes through [[bi-directional links]] and knowledge graphs, combined with BYOK white-box AI Q&A, mental model red-teaming, and a 10D research matrix to compound your knowledge into lifelong cognitive assets.

Where is my data stored, and how is my privacy protected?

All your notes, attachments, entities, and vector indexes remain 100% on your local device (SQLite and sandbox documents). There is zero registration or user tracking. AI calls connect directly to your chosen provider with keys encrypted in hardware security (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

What is BYOK, and how does AI billing work?

BYOK stands for "Bring Your Own Key". You can input API keys from OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, and more. Charges are billed directly by your model provider based on exact token usage (typically mere pennies per deep inquiry). Synapse Note adds zero markup or middleman fees.

Can I take notes completely offline without an internet connection?

Absolutely. Except for streaming queries to your configured cloud AI models, all features—Markdown editing, bi-directional linking, entity graph browsing, local full-text search, math rendering, and PDF/Word exporting—run 100% locally and offline.

How can I backup and sync my notes across multiple devices?

Your notes belong entirely to you. You can export all notes as standard Markdown with YAML frontmatter at any time, or back up the complete SQLite database snapshot, high-res PDF, or Word files. Seamless multi-device sync is supported via your private Apple iCloud container, to which we have zero access.

What is included in Synapse Note Pro, and how can I subscribe or cancel?

The core features of Synapse Note are free forever (includes 30 baseline notes; after that, you receive 5 complimentary notes every month). Pro features operate on an auto-renewing monthly subscription for only ¥19.9/month (cancel anytime), unlocking unlimited notes and star-graph connections, unlimited deep AI Agent consultations, all 6 curated knowledge packs, private cloud backup/sync, and academic-grade lossless exports. All subscriptions are processed directly through official app stores (Apple App Store / Google Play / Huawei AppGallery), where you can easily cancel anytime in your account settings.
