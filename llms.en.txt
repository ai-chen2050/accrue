# Synapse Note (fulibiji, 复利笔记, SynapseNote, SynapseNote App)

> Synapse Note is a local-first deep thinking and knowledge compounding tool. Combining Markdown bi-directional links with an entity knowledge graph, it features a white-box AI cognitive mirror, mental model red-teaming, a 10-dimensional research matrix, and publication-grade LaTeX math exports. Zero accounts, zero tracking, local-only storage, and bring-your-own-key (BYOK) privacy.

## Overview

- **Name**: Synapse Note
- **Also known as**: fulibiji, 复利笔记, SynapseNote, SynapseNote App
- **Positioning**: Local-First Bi-directional Knowledge Base & AI Cognitive Mirror
- **Platforms**: iOS / Android / macOS
- **App Store**: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6810539038

## Core Features

1. **Local-First Bi-directional Linking**: Interconnect fragmented notes like neural synapses to grow a lifelong personal knowledge network.
2. **AI Cognitive Mirror & White-box Q&A**: A private cognitive assistant grounded in your notes, zero hallucinations, with transparent reasoning chains.
3. **10-Dimensional Deep Research Matrix**: Synthesize notes into multi-angle panoramic research reports from scattered ideas to structured decisions.
4. **Mental Models & Red Team Challenge**: Apply Charlie Munger's mental model latticework; AI acts as an intellectual sparring partner.
5. **LaTeX Formula & Academic Export**: Pixel-perfect KaTeX equation rendering with direct export to publication-ready PDF and Word (.docx).
6. **Absolute Privacy & Bring Your Own Key**: 100% on-device operation, zero middleman servers, bringing your own API keys directly to providers.
