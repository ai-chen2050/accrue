Do you actually own your Second Brain—or are you just renting it?

1. The Local-First Renaissance

Across systems engineering and the indie developer community, Local-First Software has become a non-negotiable movement. Its principles are simple and uncompromising:

1. The Disk Is the Single Source of Truth: Your files live on your local filesystem first—not in a remote multi-tenant database.

2. Plain Text Forever (Future-Proof): No proprietary binary blobs. Thirty years from now, even if every software vendor today disappears, your standard .md files remain 100% readable with any text editor.

3. Offline by Default: At 35,000 feet on a plane or completely disconnected from Wi-Fi, search, graph traversal, and LaTeX rendering happen in sub-milliseconds on your device.

That ethos is the foundation of Accrue Note.

2. Inside Accrue Note's Zero-Trust Privacy Architecture

1) 100% Local Markdown & SQLite—Zero Central Servers

Open Accrue Note and you'll notice something refreshing: there is no sign-up screen. We don't even operate a backend server capable of storing your notes.

  • Every note is a standard Markdown (.md) file on your device, indexed locally for instant full-text and vector retrieval.
  • Open the exact same folder simultaneously in Obsidian, VS Code, Cursor, or Typora—zero lock-in.
  • Sync on your own terms: use your private iCloud Drive container, your own WebDAV server (Synology NAS, Nextcloud), or pure git push.

2) White-Box BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) + Local Ollama

We despise black-box AI wrappers that hide their prompts and mark up token costs by 10x. Accrue Note implements pure White-Box BYOK:

  • Plug in your own API keys for Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or DeepSeek—stored exclusively in your hardware-encrypted iOS Keychain / macOS Secure Enclave.
  • Want 100% air-gapped AI? Point Accrue Note at your local Ollama instance (running Llama 3, Qwen, or Gemma on Apple Silicon) so zero bytes ever leave your local network.
  • Radical Transparency: Inspect the exact system prompt, retrieved note citations, thinking chain, and token usage for every single AI query.

3) Fair Pricing: Free Core + Optional Lifetime Ownership

We believe software tools should respect their users' wallets:

  • Generous Free Tier: Local Markdown editing, bi-directional [[Wikilinks]], local search, and offline storage are free.
  • No Subscription Hostage: Alongside ultra-affordable monthly ($2.99/mo) and annual ($24.99/yr) Pro options, we offer a one-time Lifetime Pro unlock—pay once, own the 3D Constellation, Mental Model Sandbox, and academic export engine forever.
