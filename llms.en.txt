# Accrue (fulibiji, Accrue Notes, 复利笔记, AccrueNotes, Accrue App)

> Accrue Notes is a local-first deep thinking and knowledge compounding tool. Combining Markdown bi-directional links with an entity knowledge graph, it features a 3D interactive mindscape constellation, a white-box AI cognitive mirror, mental model red-teaming, a 10-dimensional research matrix, and publication-grade LaTeX math exports. Zero accounts, zero tracking, local-only storage, and bring-your-own-key (BYOK) privacy. Core features free; Pro subscription available in discounted Annual (¥168/yr · ~¥14/mo) and flexible Monthly (¥19.9/mo) plans with all knowledge packs included.

## Core Features

1. **Local-First Bi-directional Linking & 3D Mindscape Constellation**: Interconnect fragmented notes like neural synapses and cosmic constellations, navigating your lifelong knowledge brain in 3D.
2. **3D Spatial Math & Geometric Graphing Studio**: Interactive 3D mathematical surfaces and tangent planes calculator with real-time tuning, embedding directly into notes.
3. **3D Spatial Dissection & Holographic Digital Twin**: Transcend flat 2D text into interactive 3D anatomy for neurons, DNA supercoils, and Earth layers, featuring holographic exploded views and LiDAR scanning.
4. **AI Cognitive Mirror & White-box Q&A**: A private cognitive assistant grounded in your notes, zero hallucinations, with transparent reasoning chains.
5. **10-Dimensional Deep Research Matrix**: Synthesize notes into multi-angle panoramic research reports from scattered ideas to structured decisions.
6. **Mental Models & Red Team Challenge**: Apply Charlie Munger's mental model latticework; AI acts as an intellectual sparring partner.
7. **LaTeX Formula & Academic Export**: Pixel-perfect KaTeX equation rendering with direct export to publication-ready PDF and Word (.docx).
8. **Absolute Privacy & Bring Your Own Key**: 100% on-device operation, zero middleman servers. Core features free; Pro offers discounted Annual (¥168/yr) and Monthly (¥19.9/mo) plans.
